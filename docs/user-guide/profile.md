# Profile

## Who can use this page?

Every signed-in user can open Profile by selecting their avatar or signed-in identity in the
navigation drawer.

## Manage your photo

1. Select **Choose photo**.
2. Choose a PNG or JPEG image.
3. Wait for **Profile photo saved.**

If a photo is already set, select **Remove** to return to initials. Your username, display name,
email, and role are managed by an Admin from [Team & access](team-and-access.md); they are not editable here.

If the upload fails, keep the page open and read the error message. Try a supported image or report
the problem using [Report an issue](report-an-issue.md).

## Change your password

1. Select **Change password**.
2. Enter your current password, then your new one twice.
3. Select **Change password**.

Your new password needs at least 15 characters. A memorable phrase of a few words is both easier to
remember and harder to guess than a short scramble of symbols.

Changing your password signs you out everywhere else — your phone, a shared terminal, any browser
you forgot about. The device you are using stays signed in. If you think somebody else may have
learned your password, this is how you take the account back.

If you cannot sign in at all, an Admin can set a new password for you from
[User details](user-details.md). You will be asked to replace it the moment you sign in, because
until you do, the Admin knows it too.

### When StockControl insists

If your password was chosen by somebody else — when your account was created, or after an Admin
reset — StockControl asks for a new one before it lets you go anywhere else. This is not optional,
and it is the only screen you will see until it is done.
