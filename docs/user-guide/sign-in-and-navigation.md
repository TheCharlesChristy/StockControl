# Sign in and navigation

## Who uses this page?

Everyone signs in through `/sign-in`. You need a work email and password issued by your
StockControl administrator.

## Sign in

1. Enter your **Work email**.
2. Enter your **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.

StockControl trims spaces around the email and checks that it looks like an email address before
submitting. If the details are refused, the page shows a general message. Check both fields and
try again; the message deliberately does not identify which credential was wrong.

After a normal sign-in you land on [Overview](overview.md). If you opened a specific item or job
link while signed out, StockControl returns you to that record after sign-in.

## Move around the app

On a desktop, the left navigation drawer contains the sections available to your role. On a phone,
select **Open navigation** in the top bar to open the same drawer.

The top bar shows the current section and your role. Select your avatar or the **Signed in as**
area at the bottom of the drawer to open [Profile](profile.md). Select **Sign out** to end the
session and return to the sign-in page.

The sections are role-specific:

- **Engineer:** Overview, Jobs, Transactions, and Locations.
- **Office:** Overview, Inventory, Jobs, Stock requests, Transactions, and Locations.
- **Admin:** All Office sections plus Team & access; Locations is editable.

Everyone can open an item detail page from links or a scan, even when Inventory is not in their
navigation. The scan button is available on working pages and is hidden while the full Locations
workspace is open.

## When a page is restricted

If you open a section outside your role, StockControl shows **Access restricted** and explains that
an Admin controls access. Ask an Admin if your work requires a different role; do not share another
person’s password.
