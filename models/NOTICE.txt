The model manifest records third-party artefacts that are fetched into local
build contexts and baked into the recognition service images. Model weights
are not committed to Git.

The following entries are pinned in manifest.lock.json:

- PP-OCRv6_small_det and PP-OCRv6_small_rec official ONNX exports: Apache-2.0.
- nomic-embed-vision-v1.5 official INT8 ONNX export: Apache-2.0.
- LFM2.5-VL-1.6B Q4_0 GGUF and matching F16 multimodal projector from
  LiquidAI/LFM2.5-VL-1.6B-GGUF: LFM Open License v1.0. The upstream license is
  available at https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-VL-1.6B/blob/main/LICENSE
  and permits commercial use only where the legal entity's annual revenue is
  no more than USD 10 million, subject to the license notice and redistribution
  conditions.

The LFM files were selected by the user for a staging latency and recognition
quality comparison. The consented evaluation set and sections 19/20 accuracy,
latency, RSS, and soak gates have intentionally not been run, so this is a
reproducible provisional artifact manifest, not a model-promotion record.
