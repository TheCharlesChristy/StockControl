The model manifest records third-party artefacts that are fetched into local
build contexts and baked into the recognition service images. Model weights
are not committed to Git.

The following entries are pinned in manifest.lock.json:

- PP-OCRv6_small_det and PP-OCRv6_small_rec official ONNX exports: Apache-2.0.
- nomic-embed-vision-v1.5 official INT8 ONNX export: Apache-2.0.
- Qwen3.5-0.8B GGUF and matching F16 multimodal projector from
  unsloth/Qwen3.5-0.8B-GGUF: Apache-2.0, the same licence as the upstream
  Qwen3.5-0.8B weights it was converted from. There is no revenue threshold,
  registration step, or field-of-use restriction attached to commercial use.

Two things about that entry are deliberate and should not be treated as
oversights.

It is a community conversion, not a first-party export. Every other entry in
the manifest is published by the model's own authors, and this one is not; no
first-party GGUF of the Qwen3.5 small tier was available when it was selected.
The build still verifies each file's SHA-256 against the pinned revision, so
the artefact is reproducible, but the quantisation methodology is the
publisher's rather than the model authors'. The revision is pinned by commit
precisely because that repository has re-uploaded projector files.

It replaced LFM2.5-VL-1.6B, whose LFM Open License v1.0 permitted commercial
use only below USD 10 million annual revenue. Removing that condition was the
primary reason for the change; latency and accuracy remain unmeasured.

The consented evaluation set and sections 19/20 accuracy, latency, RSS, and
soak gates have intentionally not been run, so this is a reproducible
provisional artifact manifest, not a model-promotion record.
